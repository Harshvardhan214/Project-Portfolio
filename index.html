FAQ

Common Client Questions

Answers to the questions I'm asked most often before starting a project.

The cost depends on the type and complexity of the project. A simple business or landing page, a full e-commerce store, or a custom React.js web app all differ in pricing. After I understand your needs, I'll provide a clear, fixed quote with no hidden charges.

Most business websites are delivered within 1–2 weeks. E-commerce and larger projects typically take 2–4 weeks. I'll share a timeline before we start and keep you updated at every step.

If you need a flexible site with blogs, courses, or custom pages, WordPress is often the best fit. If you're focused on selling products quickly with built-in checkout and inventory, Shopify is ideal. I'll recommend the best platform based on your goals.

Yes — every website I build is fully responsive. It will look clean and work smoothly on desktops, tablets, and mobile phones, which is essential for user experience and SEO.

Yes. I can set up reliable hosting and connect your domain, as well as provide ongoing support and maintenance packages. You'll get a fast, secure, and always-up-to-date website.

I offer post-launch support. Each package includes a free revision period, and after that I provide affordable maintenance plans so you can request content updates or new features anytime.
